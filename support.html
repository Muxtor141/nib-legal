Help & Support

Quick answers to the things people ask most.

Fill or Draw — what's the difference?

In Fill mode, tap an area to fill it with your marker color. In Draw mode, color freehand like a real marker. Apple Pencil always draws, whichever mode you're in. Fills stop at the line art, so they never leak outside the lines.

What are the gel pen and the eraser?

The gel pen (the first pen in the tray) draws opaque ink on top of your markers, for white highlights, sparkles and tiny details. The eraser next to Fill and Draw clears one area back to bare paper when you tap it.

What are Kraft and Midnight paper?

Tap the selected marker to open brush settings, then pick a paper. Kraft is warm brown paper where markers look muted and white gel pops. Midnight is dark paper where colors add up like light and bright fills glow. Both come with Nib Plus.

How do I zoom in?

Pinch with two fingers to zoom and move the page. A pinch never paints, so you won't leave stray marks.

Is my work saved?

Yes, automatically. Every stroke and fill is saved the instant you make it, even if the app closes. Use undo and redo to step back and forward through your changes.

How do I save an image or a timelapse?

When you're done, use Save Image to add the picture to Photos, or export a timelapse video that replays your coloring stroke by stroke. Share either one straight to any app. Free exports carry a small "Nib" mark; Nib Plus removes it and adds HD export.

Where is my artwork stored, and how do I back it up?

Only on your device — it's never uploaded to us. To keep a copy, use Settings → Export all art. Deleting the app deletes its artwork, so export first. See the Privacy Policy.

What stays free?

A free selection of pages, a free daily page and all core tools. Nib Plus adds every page and book, all marker sets and papers, HD export and no "Nib" mark.

What happens to my pages if Plus ends?

Nothing you've started gets locked. Any page you've begun stays colorable forever, even if your subscription ends.

Restore or cancel a purchase

On a new device, open Nib's Settings and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account. To cancel a subscription, go to iOS Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions at least 24 hours before it renews. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Still stuck?

Open a support request and describe what happened. Include your device model and iOS version if something isn't working.
