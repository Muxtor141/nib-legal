Privacy Policy

Effective 23 October 2026

The short version: your artwork stays on your device. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

What never leaves your device

  • Your artwork is stored only on your device. Colored pages, their stroke history and thumbnails are kept in the app's private storage. They are not uploaded to us or to anyone else.
  • Photos you turn into pages never leave your device. Nib makes the page on your iPhone or iPad and keeps only the finished drawing, not the photo.
  • Coloring, saving and exporting work fully offline.
  • Nib has no accounts and no sign-in, and contains no ads, no advertising SDKs, no third-party analytics SDKs and no tracking.

Photo pages ("Color your photo")

When you choose a photo to turn into a coloring page, Apple's photo picker gives Nib only that one photo; Nib can't see the rest of your library. The page is made entirely on your device: Apple's on-device Vision framework finds the subject (and, for pets and faces, the eyes), and Nib's own code draws the lines. The photo is not uploaded, not sent to us or anyone else, and not saved by Nib. Only the finished page (its line drawing, its areas and a small set of colors taken from the photo) is saved in the app's private storage, like your artwork.

Imported pages

When you import a coloring page (a PNG, JPG or PDF from Files, or a photo of a printed page), Nib cleans up its lines on your device so every area can be filled. The file or photo is not uploaded, not sent to us or anyone else, and not saved by Nib: only the cleaned-up page is saved in the app's private storage. Please import only pages you have the right to use.

Coloring pages from our content server

Nib ships with built-in pages. It may also download new pages and an updated page list from our content server, which is a static file host. These are plain downloads: like any web request, they expose your IP address and basic request information (such as the time of the request and standard network headers) to the host. No personal data and no artwork is sent.

Purchases

Nib Plus purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple. To validate them and restore them on your other devices we use RevenueCat, which receives a random anonymous app user identifier, the purchase and transaction information Apple provides, and basic technical details (app version, OS version, device model, country or storefront). RevenueCat never receives your artwork, and we don't send it your name or email.

Permissions

  • Notifications (optional) — a reminder when the free daily page is ready. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device; there is no push server.
  • Photos (add only) — requested only when you tap Save Image or save a video, so Nib can add it to your library. Nib cannot read your library.
  • Photo picker and Files — used for photo pages, imports and "Me vs. you". They need no permission: Apple's pickers hand Nib only the photo or file you choose.
  • Nib does not use the camera, location, contacts or microphone.

Sharing

When you share an image or timelapse video, it goes to the app or service you choose. What happens next is governed by that app or service. Exports from the free version carry a small "Nib" mark.

Retention and deletion

Your artwork stays until you delete it. Delete a single artwork in My Art (long press → Delete), a photo page and everything colored on it in the Library (long press → Delete page), or delete the app to remove everything. Images and videos you've already saved to Photos or shared elsewhere are not affected.

Children

Nib is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
