Bold & easy coloring for grown-ups.

Nib is a calm coloring book for iPhone and iPad, with digital markers that feel real — tap to fill or color freehand — and a timelapse of every page you finish.

Never lose work

Every stroke and fill is saved the moment you make it, even if the app closes. Your art stays on your device — it is never uploaded to us.

Works offline

Coloring, saving and exporting all work without a connection. No account, no sign-in, no ads and no tracking.

Fair pricing, never locked

Free pages, a free daily page and every core tool cost nothing. Nib Plus unlocks the rest — and any page you've started stays colorable forever, even if Plus ends.
